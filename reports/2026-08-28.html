Section 03 · Analyst notes

Today's takeaways

Gamescom's second hardware day books five cards, and the shape of the day is one vendor going wide and one going deliberately cheap. Turtle Beach took three of the five off a single Aug 26 line-up drop: the Vulcan II XP at $149.99 for Oct 25, a full-size wired board that finally makes the Vulcan chassis hot-swap on both 3-pin and 5-pin with pre-lubed TITAN HS switches and ReacTap SOCD; the Victrix Pro BFG Reloaded Fortnite Drift editions across PS, Xbox and PC for Oct 8 — notable mainly because the licence carries no upcharge, holding $209.99/$189.99; and two licensed Switch 2 Rematch Wireless RGB pads at $69.99, the Epic-and-Nintendo co-licensed “Kevin the Cube” on Oct 8 and a lenticular Minecraft Dungeons II unit on Sept 26. All three ride TMR or Hall-effect anti-drift as table stakes now, not as a differentiator. CHERRY XTRFY went the other way and it is the more interesting card. The M42V2 is 45 g, solid-shell, two tool-free swappable back shells, no software — and a PixArt 3327 capped at 1 kHz, which in 2026 is three sensor generations back. That is not an oversight; it is the whole product. At €39.99, on sale the same day it was announced, CHERRY is buying shape choice and weight at a price where nobody cross-shops polling rate, with HeatoN's name on it for credibility. Set that against the week's other mice: ASUS put a 65K sensor and 8 kHz into the Gladius IV Ace on Aug 26, HyperX wants $129.99 for a 47 g Pulsefire Haste 3 Pro. The sub-€50 ultralight tier is now contested by a switch brand, and Logitech's answer there is the G305 X SUPERLIGHT at $79.99 — twice the price, heavier, and two months older. Thrustmaster is the only sim card and it is a niche extension, not a range move: the SimTask Truck-40, a 400 mm rim with 22 buttons and a metal support that lays the wheel over four height positions and 30° of tilt, for October, no price. It fits any Thrustmaster base with a detachable QR but the mount excludes T128, T248, T248R and T98 — i.e. most of the volume tier. Worth reading next to PXN's W TS 400 mm Scania wheel from the Aug 24 release: two vendors independently deciding truck sim is worth a dedicated SKU in the same week. Five exclusions are recorded rather than smoothed. Xbox's X25 general pre-order opened Aug 27 — a retail door, not an announcement; the SKU was booked Aug 20 and again Aug 26. Turtle Beach's Command MC7/MC5/MC3 mice appear in the same Gamescom release with firm dates (MC3 and MC7 Aug 30, MC5 Sept 20) but the Command Series was announced Apr 23; a ship date is not a launch. Epomaker's Gamescom debut is dated Aug 26 but its HE60/HE108/TMR boards and Nex mice were carded here two days ago. SIMAGIC's TB-Neo load-cell handbrake lands Aug 25 — three days out, just past the 48-hour line.
