Section 03 · Analyst notes

Today's takeaways

Gamescom trade day is one licensing programme wearing ten hats: six of today's ten cards are the Designed for XBOX 25th Anniversary Collection, with Razer (mouse + keyboard), GameSir, Backbone and Microsoft's own X25 pad all shipping the same translucent-green story to an Oct 20 date. Logitech G is absent from that collection entirely — no Designed-for-Xbox anniversary SKU, no Gamescom hardware — because it is holding everything for G PLAY on Sept 23, which promises sim-racing reveals. Worth watching whether a four-week gap behind an Xbox-branded retail wave costs anything in Q4 shelf space. On raw spec, ASUS is the one to flag to product: the Gladius IV Ace pairs 8 kHz wireless with a 56 g ergonomic shell and a desk-side receiver, which is both lighter and faster than Logitech's current top ergonomic SKU. Two housekeeping notes — Epomaker is not in sources.md (third brand this month after PXN and G-Wolves; it debuted at Gamescom with a full HE/TMR lineup and its first mouse, so it should be booked), and the X25 pad is deliberately re-listed after the Aug 20 leak card because today is the official listing and pre-order open, not a recycle.
