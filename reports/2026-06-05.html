Mice 1 item

Keychron

MagOptic switch + G-series HE mice

Mouse

Computex floor reveal (Jun 3 PC Gamer / Jun 3 GAMES.GG): Keychron's hybrid MagOptic mouse switch is shipping with a built-in haptic engine — explicitly framed by CEO Nick Xu as "give people both" in response to the Superstrike's pure-haptic critique. Mechanical leaf-spring physical click + a sensor stage that toggles in software between optical mode (traditional click) and Hall-effect magnetic mode (adjustable actuation + Rapid Trigger). Three switch variants (clicky / tactile / linear), all delivering real mechanical click feedback, not simulated. Xu's quote at the booth: "We combine this with a haptic engine — you know, just a vibrator — inside the mouse, then it's just same trick." First products are the G-series HE mice (same "HE" suffix used on the Hall-effect keyboard line), expected end of June 2026 — first competitor to Logitech's HITS-driven Superstrike that gives buyers an explicit toggle between the two click modalities.
