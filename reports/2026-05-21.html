Section 03 · Analyst notes

Today's takeaways

Two parallel pressures on Logitech this week. Hardware-side, Razer's Viper V4 Pro is now the reference 49 g / 8K / FrameSync flagship that the Superstrike has to be compared against — and the comparison is increasingly about value, not feel. Software/QA-side, the Superlight family's switch-failure narrative is louder than any single new spec; the highest-volume wishlist item is "better warranty" not "more polling rate." Worth raising to product: Logitech's new Pro X TKL Rapid undercuts Wooting/Razer on price, which is the playbook that could work for the mouse side too.
