Mouse

Yesterday's "Shaped by Light" recap is now backed by real numbers. Finalmouse posted the official Starlight X announcement, the SLX Nightfall product page is live on finalmouse.com, and tech press has the spec sheet: 38 g carbon-fiber composite shell (124.8 × 58.9 × 39.5 mm), Finalmouse's first new shape in 11 years (symmetrical, fuller rear hump, fewer top cutouts for rigidity), in-house F1 sensor co-developed with PixArt, Nordic nRF54LM20 MCU, and the headline TMR-DS™ ("TMR Dual-State") click system — magnetic analog actuation paired alongside a traditional mechanical click for tactile feedback, with adjustable actuation in 0.01 mm increments (40 discrete steps vs Logitech Superstrike's 10).
