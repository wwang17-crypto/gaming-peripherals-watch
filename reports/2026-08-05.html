Section 03 · Analyst notes

Today's takeaways

Wednesday breaks the four-day 0/0/0/0 run, but on the smallest brand on the mouse list rather than a priority one. Pwnage's Vanta is the only priority-list SKU with a fresh in-window source, and its pitch is a direct shot at the differentiation problem the whole category is now fighting over: instead of a new switch technology, it makes the existing SPDT switch mechanically tunable — click force, resistance, force curve, break point and return, all adjustable with the bundled screwdriver, plus a sliding sensor position. That is the same "the click itself is the product" thesis Logitech spent $179.99 on with the Superstrike's Haptic Inductive Trigger System in February, arriving here at $159 from a boutique with no software dependency at all — a useful reminder that the moat around HITS is narrower than the launch narrative implied. Keyboards, Sim Gear and Controllers land honestly empty on a verified per-brand recency sweep (Logitech G, Razer, Corsair, SteelSeries, Wooting, ASUS ROG, Glorious, Pulsar, Lamzu, Zowie, Finalmouse, WLmouse, VAXEE, Fanatec, MOZA, Thrustmaster, Simagic, Asetek, Simucube, Heusinkveld, Cammus, Xbox, PlayStation, Nintendo, 8BitDo, GameSir, SCUF, Turtle Beach, Backbone, Hori): everything surfaced fails on date, type or scope — Pulsar's Feinmann F01 Noctua Edition shipped Jul 21, Asetek's shifter and handbrake are a May 22 OverTake report pointing at a Gamescom window with no date or final price, MOZA's R16 Ultra / HMA150 / R5 Pro were booked 07-30, Sim-Lab's DDS TorqueSync was ruled out-of-scope 08-02, and 8BitDo Ultimate 3E, Cherry XTRFY K63W Pro Compact and GameSir T7 Pro Retro Green remain previously-announced SKUs with August retail doors. The Logitech-side event of the day is calendar, not catalog: an Aug 4 Lausanne press release anchored G PLAY 2026's European leg in Warsaw — EXPO XXI, House of G free and open to the public Sept 22–23, Connect CS2 tournament Sept 23 at 18:00 CET, invite-only keynote at Art Box, global livestream Sept 23 at 10:00 AM PST / 19:00 CET, and Deal Days across Europe Sept 16 – Oct 2 at up to 40% off including sim racing gear. That is an event announcement, so it stays out of Section 1 per the Fanatec Silverstone precedent — but two details matter for product. First, it revises the Jul 29 global release, moving the Warsaw tournament from Sept 22 to Sept 23 and the broadcast from 9:00 AM to 10:00 AM PST, which reads as a slate still being assembled six weeks out. Second, the Warsaw copy explicitly stages sim-racing rigs alongside real McLaren F1 cars — the second first-party signal in a week that sim racing is carrying weight in the reveal, against a catalog that still has no entry-DD wheel below the RS50 and no magnetic or Hall-effect keyboard anywhere. Actionable read: the competitive floor keeps moving while the answer stays parked on Sept 23 — Razer's $119.99 HE board still undercuts the G316 X 98 on features at identical price, MOZA's $399 R5 Pro bundle still sits in the geared-G923 band, and now a $159 boutique mouse is selling adjustable click feel without haptics or a driver.
