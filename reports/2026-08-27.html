Section 03 · Analyst notes

Today's takeaways

Gamescom's first public day is MOZA converting three weeks of teasers into four cards. Mercedes-Benz, Ford Mustang GTD and the MotoGP handlebar all landed with specs; the Porsche 911 GT3 RS is deliberately not re-carded because yesterday already booked it. Only one of the four has a price: the €45 PHF haptic pedal kit, shipping mid-September, and it is the most commercially interesting of the set — a cheap retrofit that adds ABS, lock-up and slip cues to MOZA's entire installed base of passive pedals rather than selling a new pedal set. Logitech has no equivalent add-on economy at all: the RS ecosystem still has no third-party QR, no accessory upgrade path, and now a competitor monetising hardware customers already own for the price of two games. On keyboards, ASUS put an analog board in the TUF tier — K4 Magnetic at 0.1–3.3 mm and 8 kHz — which pressures Logitech's floor from below, since its only analog board remains the wired G512 X at $179.99. Three exclusions worth recording rather than smoothing.
