Sharkoon

SKILLER SGM25W · 59 g dual-mode wireless at €24.99

Mouse

Sharkoon dropped the SKILLER SGM25W — a 59 g symmetric wireless mouse with PixArt PAW3311 (12,000 DPI), 2.4 GHz + USB-C dual-mode, 40h battery and PTFE skates — at €24.99. That's aggressive: lightweight wireless has historically lived in the $100–160 segment, and Sharkoon is undercutting it 5× while still hitting 59 g and dual-mode. Available in standard black and an "Ocean" blue-wave colourway (identical hardware). Polling caps at 1,000 Hz — no 4K/8K story here — but for the price, it's the new floor for "wireless ultralight."

Finalmouse

Centerpiece Pro Founders Edition · "Shaped by Light" reveal lands

Keyboard

Yesterday's (May 25, 11:00 PT) "Shaped by Light" livestream was — finally — the long-delayed Centerpiece Pro Founders Edition shipping. Finalmouse's first keyboard: 65% layout, patented Hall-effect KeySwitch (co-engineered with Gateron), 0.1 mm analog actuation with rapid trigger, true 8 kHz polling, and the marquee feature — a "Laminated DisplayCircuit Glass Stack" screen under the keys that runs interactive skins (anime, Halo, CS2), togglable legends, and video plugins. Numbered 1/17000 collector run for Founders Edition; standard edition follows. $349, 3 years late, fans calling it the long-rumoured "holy grail." Reception split: skin tech genuinely novel, $349 vs delays narrative remains.

Thrustmaster

T818 Black Edition · US release confirmed at SimRacing Expo Charlotte

Sim

SimRacing Expo Charlotte (May 22–24, just ended) was Thrustmaster's first US public hands-on of the T818 Black Edition — the reworked T818 with reinforced motor + connectors, all-black colourway, and a $50 price drop to $649.99 (vs €599.99 in Europe, where it shipped late 2025). 10 Nm constant torque, no saturation, same wheel/rim ecosystem.
