  • G915 X / G915 X TKL

    Tom's Hardware Forum thread this week documents the recurring G915 X key-chatter / double-typing failure across three RMA replacement units — same user, three keyboards, all developed bouncing keys within 2–6 months on different rows (q → w → a/d, then space + numeric row on a third). One user's workaround is enabling Windows FilterKeys with custom registry values, though FilterKeys treats debounced presses as releases (problematic in games). This is the exact failure mode the G915 X refresh was supposed to fix — Logitech told PC Gamer the engineers "looked at the community feedback" on the original G915 before the X redesign, but the chatter complaints have followed onto the new SKU.
