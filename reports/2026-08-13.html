Today's takeaways

Thursday closes a thread this report has carried open since Aug 8: the Storm King controller is real, and it is exactly the paint job the leak described. Xbox Wire published at Aug 12 — seven days after billbil-kun's Dealabs post, and after four consecutive editions logged “still no official reveal” as a disqualifier. The leak was accurate on every material point: $89.99 / £84.99, matching the Forza Horizon 6 pad; cracked-asphalt shell with red glowing fissures over the Crimson Omen; blacked-out A/B/Y with a transparent red X; red triggers and a transparent D-pad. Two details the leak did not have: a metallic COG tag on the battery door carrying “Never Fight Alone”, and a second Design Lab configuration with a skull-over-crossed-Lancers top case by heavy-metal illustrator Luke Preece, opening Sep 30 with metallic triggers, rubberised grips and engraving as paid add-ons. Pre-orders opened the same day; the pad ships Sep 30, six days before E-Day's Oct 6 launch, and both controllers get their first public showing at the Xbox booth at Gamescom. Booked as one card, not two — the Design Lab version has no standalone price and no hardware delta — and booked as a colourway, the same call made for the Aug 6 DualSense Icon Blue and the Aug 11 Wolverine pair. Nothing about it is a hardware change: no HE or TMR sticks, no back paddles, no latency or connectivity claim. Notably, there is still no Elite variant for what is Xbox's biggest release of the year, which is drawing more comment than the design itself.
